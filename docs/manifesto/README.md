# Manifesto

Product manifesto — vision, principles, and non-negotiables. The manifesto defines the product's identity and guiding philosophy.

## What Goes Here

- Product manifesto
- Vision statement
- Core principles
- Non-negotiable values

## What Does NOT Go Here

- Product requirements → `../prd/`
- Architecture decisions → `../adr/`
- MVP definitions → `../mvp/`

## Naming Convention

| Type | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Manifesto | `<product>-manifesto.md` | `anvil-manifesto.md` |

## Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Product Owner | Defines product vision and principles |
| Founders/Leadership | Approve manifesto |

## Related Folders

- `../prd/` — PRDs are guided by the manifesto
- `../mvp/` — MVP scope aligns with manifesto principles
- `../specification-corpus/` — Shared terminology and definitions
