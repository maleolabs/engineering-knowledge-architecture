## A.2 Stabilization Verdict

- **No architectural reversal**: all core decisions (dual-layer + Exchange, identity `(Namespace, Type, ID, InstanceVersion)`, 5 state domains, single-writer, 7 invariants) survive intact.
- **1 critical + 7 major + 5 minor** — all fixes are definitional and language-consistency fixes; no new concepts introduced (the "new" terms — Change Log, Identity Registry, Trigger, well-formed, Knowledge OS — are only explicitations of already-used terms).
- **Verdict: RATIFIED WITH AMENDMENTS** — EKA v1.0 declared stable and fit for publication after applying the 13 resolutions above.
